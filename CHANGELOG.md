# Recent changes

This is a short public summary of Melancholy's recent application releases, based on the private project's release notes. Run `/changelogs` in Discord for the full notes. Since 2.15, versions read as v2.15 instead of v2.15.0; patch releases keep their last number.

## 2.18.3 · This month, clearer usage

XP and voice leaderboards can be sorted by this month. Usage errors show the full command to type, an example where one fits, and what the symbols mean, and command usage reads more clearly across `!!help`.

## 2.18.2 · Shorter commands, bigger boards

Every prefix command has a short alias, and many options and flags have short forms too. `!!shop` does everything `/shop` can. `/music global` ranks linked Last.fm accounts by their plays of an artist, and `/music privacy` chooses whether your name shows. Voice has its own leaderboard, and boards can be sorted highest to lowest, lowest to highest, this week, or today. Level cards, profiles, and badges got a cleaner look with new pixel icons and country flags. Fixed voice time being lost when moving between channels.

## 2.18.1 · Games that follow the chat

`/music top` and `!!music top` work without a username, using your linked Last.fm account. Would you rather and word chain move to the bottom of the chat when they update after new messages. Fixed word chain making other players wait while someone had the add a word form open.

## 2.18 · Birthdays

`/birthday` keeps one birthday across every server, celebrated from midnight in your own timezone. Birthday cards show the countdown, the age you're turning, your zodiac sign, birthstone, birth flower, and birthday twins in the server. Friends can leave sealed wishes up to 30 days early. On the day, opening them gives a gem gift that grows with every wish, while your cake grows from a cupcake to a towering party cake. Servers can announce birthdays and give a birthday role for the day.

Voice leveling added a second level track beside message xp, up to level 100, with voice xp for every 2 minutes spent talking with others. Also added: `!!level` as a prefix version of the level card, with admin xp changes, and `!!games` with every `/games` game plus shortcuts like `!!dice` and `!!wyr`. Level up messages became a minimal panel that no longer pings level roles. XP and message counts got new pixel icons.

## 2.17.1 · Melancholy everywhere

Thirteen commands, including `/game`, `/music`, `/anime`, `/weather`, and `/avatar`, work through a user install in DMs, group chats, and servers Melancholy isn't in. osu! beatmaps can show the map's top 5. `/music leaderboard` ranks linked server members. Social actions, `/info user` badges, weather, and many utility panels got pixel icons and compact layouts. All icons became the bot's own application emojis.

## 2.17 · Game stats

`/game` shows stats for osu!, Chess.com, Lichess, Dota 2, and PUBG: profiles, recent and top plays, ratings, matches, scoreboards, and leaderboards. Members can link one account per game. The game panels added 95 pixel icons.

## 2.16 · Last.fm and anime icons, and a new welcome

`/music nowplaying` shows the song you're playing now. The music profile was redesigned, and who knows works for songs and albums. Music and anime panels got matching animated pixel icons. The message Melancholy sends when it joins a server was rewritten. The minigame weapon system was removed.

## 2.15 · Reworked info commands

`/info app`, `server`, `role`, and `user` became compact panels. `/editrole` was added for staff. Profile badges, charm hearts, and activity tiers got animated pixel art. The minigame skills system was removed.

## 2.14 · Reworked server logs

Server logs became compact panels that show exactly what changed. Bulk deletes attach a transcript, join logs flag new accounts, and leave logs show kicks. Logs can no longer ping members or roles. 2.14.1 added new coin and gem icons.

## 2.13 · Shop rework

The shop split into server, global, level card, and profile card shelves under `/shop browse`, with item pages, ratings, and bulk buying. New item types include temporary roles, xp boosters, mystery boxes, work refreshes, redeemables, and collectibles. 2.13.1 added loading screens, pixel icons, a complete-all-work option, and redesigned work and cooldown panels.

## 2.12 · Pixel-art cards

All leaderboards moved into one `/leaderboard` image card with a podium. The minigame, level card, and profile card were redrawn as pixel-art cards. `/changelogs` gained a version menu.

## 2.11 · Last.fm music

`/music` added Last.fm browsing, charts, recent scrobbles, and personal top charts. 2.11.1 improved performance in busy servers. 2.11.2 added account linking, who knows, and prefix shorthands.

## 2.10.4 · Role creation

`/createrole` accepts many color formats and gradient or holographic role colors where the server supports them.

## 2.10.3 · Level stats UI update

Level stats opens with a level card and an option to show an extended card with nearby ranked members.

## Earlier

2.10.2 added seven social actions. 2.10.0 and 2.10.1 expanded and sped up anime search. 2.9.9 added `/home` and `/help find`. 2.9.8 added guild jobs and role income. 2.9.6 updated AFK and Starlight.
