# Recent changes

This is a short public summary of Melancholy's recent application releases, based on the private project's release notes. The public display repository has its own future commit history.

## 2.10.3 · Level stats UI update

Level stats opens with a level card and an option to show an extended card. The extended view includes nearby ranked members, progress, and clearer number formatting.

## 2.10.2 · More social actions

Seven actions were added: clap, handshake, carry, peck, blowkiss, thumbsup, and facepalm. Action statistics gained pagination across all 37 actions.

## 2.10.1 · Faster anime browsing

Anime search loads optional details and streaming availability for the page being viewed. Exact autocomplete selections and visited pages avoid unnecessary repeat searches.

## 2.10.0 · Expanded anime search

Search gained separate paths for series, characters, studios, and people. Series pages added cast, related titles, trailers, and watch options when available.

## 2.9.9 · Home and command finding

`/home` added personal shortcuts and reward readiness. `/help find` added searches by command name or a short goal.

## 2.9.8 · Guild jobs and role income

Server staff gained custom occupations and recurring role income. Starlight quests gained separate daily, weekly, and seasonal pages.

## 2.9.6 · AFK and Starlight updates

AFK controls gained set, list, and remove actions. Starlight changed its season rollover and added inactivity decay after a grace period.
